How to record

1

Install Sensor Logger

Free on Android and iPhone. Switch on Accelerometer, Gravity, Gyroscope, Location and Annotation, and "Standardise Units & Frames".

2

Start and sync

Press Start, stand still for 10 seconds, then press the Annotation button firmly three times.

3

Walk normally

Hold the phone flat, screen up. Press 1 right after stepping up a kerb, 2 after stepping down. Double-press 1 before stairs up, 2 before stairs down.

4

Finish

Press Annotation three times again, stand still for 10 seconds, Stop, and export as Zipped CSV.

About PathSense

Navigation apps give the shortest route, but they don't know about kerbs, stairs or broken paths. PathSense maps them from the motion sensors in ordinary phones carried by people walking around campus, and plans routes for wheelchair users, people on crutches and people with low vision.

How it works

F1

Surface sensing

Kerbs, slope and roughness from the phone's accelerometer and gravity readings.

F2

Many walks, one map

Walks are combined; a kerb counts once two walks agree, and each contributor is weighed.

F3

Learning without labels

A contrastive (SimCLR) encoder learns from unlabelled walks.

F4

Any phone

Domain-adversarial training so iPhone and Android data look alike.

F5

Filling the gaps

A graph network estimates paths nobody has walked yet (research model; the site itself shows unchecked paths as unknown).

F6

Routes per person

A* search with costs for each mobility profile; stairs are impossible for wheelchairs.

F7

Tuned for walking

Signal processing tuned for a phone held in the hand.
